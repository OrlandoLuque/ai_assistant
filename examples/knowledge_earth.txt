The Earth is the third planet from the Sun in our solar system.
Earth orbits the Sun at an average distance of approximately 149.6 million kilometers.
The planet has a diameter of approximately 12,742 kilometers.
Earth has one natural satellite called the Moon.
The Moon orbits Earth at an average distance of 384,400 kilometers.
Earth's atmosphere is composed primarily of nitrogen (78%) and oxygen (21%).
The remaining 1% of the atmosphere includes argon, carbon dioxide, and trace gases.
Earth completes one full rotation on its axis every 23 hours and 56 minutes.
Earth completes one orbit around the Sun every 365.25 days.
The Moon influences Earth's ocean tides through gravitational pull.
Earth's surface is approximately 71% water and 29% land.
The planet has a magnetic field generated by its liquid iron-nickel outer core.
Earth's inner core is solid iron at approximately 5,200 degrees Celsius.
Earth is the only known planet confirmed to support life.
The highest point on Earth is Mount Everest at 8,849 meters above sea level.
The deepest point is the Mariana Trench at approximately 10,994 meters below sea level.
Earth's mass is approximately 5.97 x 10^24 kilograms.
The planet's axial tilt is 23.5 degrees, which causes seasons.
Earth formed approximately 4.54 billion years ago.
